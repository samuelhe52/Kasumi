# Native Mathematics

Inline formulas follow the text baseline: $E=mc^2$, $x_i^2$, and
$\alpha+\beta=\gamma$. A fraction $\frac{1}{3}$ or root $\sqrt{x}$ can make
the line taller while the surrounding words remain selectable.

## Fractions, roots, and delimiters

$$
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
\sqrt[3]{x} + \sqrt{\frac{1}{3}} = \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)
$$

## Operators and matrices

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

$$
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}x\\y\end{bmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix}ax+by\\cx+dy\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Alphabets, accents, and colors

$$
\mathbb{R} \supset \mathbb{Q} \supset \mathbb{Z},\quad
\mathbf{v} + \vec{x},\quad \hat{a},\quad \overline{AB}
$$

$$
\color{red}{\frac{a}{b}} + \color{blue}{\sin x}
$$

Search for `alpha` or `frac` in the rendered reader. Copying a formula returns
its TeX, including the command backslashes and grouping braces.
