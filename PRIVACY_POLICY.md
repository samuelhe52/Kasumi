# Kasumi Markdown Privacy Policy

Kasumi Markdown is a Markdown reader and editor for iPhone and iPad. You can use it without an account. Kasumi does not include advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics or crash reporting.

## Documents and preferences

Kasumi reads, renders, searches, and edits your documents on your device. It keeps app preferences and reading state on the device. You choose where to save your files and exports. If you use iCloud Drive or another Files provider, that provider handles storage and synchronization for files you save there. The Import into Kasumi extension uses shared storage on your device to pass a file to the app.

When you choose to paste from the clipboard, Kasumi reads the text to create a local Markdown document. You can remove documents through the document browser or Files, and remove downloaded fonts in Kasumi’s font settings.

## Network requests

When you open a document by URL or render a document with remote images, Kasumi requests those resources from their hosts. Optional font downloads contact Apple or GitHub. When you follow a link, the destination handles your visit under its own privacy policy. Services contacted by these features may receive your IP address, the requested URL, and standard network request information.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [Kasumi support page](https://github.com/samuelhe52/Kasumi/issues).
