two-face 0.5.2+bat-0.26.1: MIT OR Apache-2.0; CosmicHorror/CosmicHarper.
https://codeberg.org/CosmicHarper/two-face
Uses the bat-curated precompiled grammar bundle with the pure-Rust fancy-regex
backend. Grammar authors have separate notices preserved in GRAMMAR-LICENSES.md.
No two-face themes are used.

All exact package versions and registry checksums are recorded in Cargo.lock
and dependencies.json. Complete registry license files, including build-time
packages, are preserved in CODE-LICENSES.txt. The registry sources are unmodified;
Kasumi's bridge adds language aliases, palette categories, UTF-16 mapping, and
input-size guards.
